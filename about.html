Project Objectives

This digital expo exhibit is designed to achieve three primary goals. First, it aims to demystify complex scientific phenomena regarding electromagnetic waves for the general public. Second, it educates visitors on the financial realities of grocery shopping and product sourcing. Third, it promotes well-balanced lifestyle routines that foster both physical and digital wellness in our hyper-connected local community.

Project Purpose

The primary purpose of this website is to serve as an interactive, multi-disciplinary learning platform. By bridging the gap between physics, economics, and health sciences, we empower everyday consumers to make practical, data-driven decisions. This exhibit proves that scientific theory and financial planning directly affect our daily habits, physical safety, and personal nutrition choices.


Target Audience

Our digital showcase is intentionally structured to inform and support three key target groups:

  • Students and Educators: Individuals seeking clear, local, and academic connections between health guidelines and market forces.
  • Everyday Household Shoppers: Consumers aiming to optimize their physical health and household food budgets simultaneously.
  • Responsible Tech Users: Community members striving to balance heavy daily device usage with safety-first digital behavior habits.
