Lifestyle Guidelines for Wellness

Achieving a healthy life requires consistency across physical nutrition, daily exercise, and mental rejuvenation. Follow these foundational pillars for balanced well-being:

  • Nutrient-Dense Diet: Prioritize whole foods like leafy greens, lean proteins, and complex carbohydrates while minimizing artificial sugars and processed snacks.
  • Physical Activity: Aim for at least 30 minutes of moderate exercise daily, such as brisk walking, cycling, or bodyweight training, to support cardiovascular health.
  • Hydration and Rest: Drink a minimum of 8 glasses of water daily and secure 7 to 9 hours of restorative sleep to allow muscle recovery and mental clarity.
  • Digital Detoxing: Establish dedicated device-free hours before bedtime to limit non-ionizing blue light exposure and improve overall sleep quality.
