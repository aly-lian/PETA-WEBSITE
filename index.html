Project Overview

Welcome to our Healthy Living Digital Expo exhibit website by Grade 9 - OLO Chartres Discipline Committee! This interdisciplinary capstone project bridges the fundamental concepts of physics, lifestyle diseases, and consumer awareness. Our goal is to explore how Electromagnetic (EM) Radiation impacts modern wellness, how technology can promote sustainable health practices, and how young citizens can make informed, data-driven decisions as smart consumers in today's digital landscape.

Sustainable Development Goals

Our digital exhibit directly aligns with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals to foster global citizenship and holistic health:

  • SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being – Raising awareness on preventable non-communicable diseases, balanced nutrition, and the biological risks of prolonged ionizing and non-ionizing radiation.
  • SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production – Empowering consumers to critically evaluate product labels, budgeting, and the hidden science behind daily wellness commodities.
