Understanding Multimodal Literacy

A multimodal text combines two or more communication modes to convey a unified meaning. It merges written language, visual imagery, and structural design to make information more engaging and accessible. For our digital expo, we have crafted a multimodal presentation that bridges scientific facts about electromagnetic radiation with practical consumer budgeting to advocate for holistic community wellness.

Visual Storyboard & Media Campaign Design

Below is the structural framework of our multimodal awareness campaign, detailing how print materials, imagery, and text work together to educate our peers:

  • Campaign Poster 1: The Invisible Waves
    Visual Elements: A striking graphic of a person tracking daily screen time, overlaid with color-coded frequency bars representing non-ionizing waves.
    Text Component: "Disconnect to Reconnect. Be mindful of your digital habits and limit close-range radiation exposure."

  • Campaign Poster 2: The Healthy Wallet
    Visual Elements: A side-by-side comparison chart showing the affordability of locally sourced, nutrient-dense ingredients versus expensive processed food packages.
    Text Component: "Smart Choices, Better Health. Apply market economics to fuel your body without emptying your pockets."

  • Campaign Poster 3: The Digital Balance
    Visual Elements: A student gracefully shutting a laptop lid and stepping into a natural, open-air park setting under pure sunlight.
    Text Component: "Practice True Digital Citizenship. Protect your physical safety and mental clarity by embracing life offline."
