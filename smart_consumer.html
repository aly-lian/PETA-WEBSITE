Demand and Supply Concepts

In a free-market economy, prices are determined by the interaction of buyers and sellers. As a smart consumer, recognizing these forces helps you make better purchasing decisions and budget your household income effectively:

  • The Law of Demand: Holding all else constant, as the price of a healthy good decreases, consumer demand for that item increases. If prices rise, consumers buy less.
  • The Law of Supply: Holding all else constant, as the market price of an item increases, producers are motivated to supply more of it to maximize profit margins.
  • Market Equilibrium: This occurs when the quantity demanded by consumers matches the exact quantity supplied by expo sellers, establishing a stable market price.

Practical Consumer Budgeting Strategy

Smart consumers leverage market data to avoid inflation traps. When health food shortages drive local prices up, buyers can adapt by shifting demand toward equivalent generic brands or seasonal alternatives until market equilibrium restores affordable regular pricing structures.
